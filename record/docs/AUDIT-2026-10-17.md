## 6. Assessment: the open questions that would most move the family balance

These are based only on the files above.

1. **Does any single producer pass the joint board without per-metric tuning (the "j" joint producer)?**
   - Today every entrant fails the v1 bands (g0, g1, h1).
   - m4's draft rule prefers G by 2 outs, but by that same rule all three families meet the kill precondition. The bands no control family reaches are line-edge JSD and edge coupling.
   - The two closest candidates fail on opposite bands. The G1 circular list misses h2 and JSD. The H1 flow cipher misses the line edges and repeats.
   - A pass by the flow cipher plus a line mechanism plus a hapax-heavy table would favour verbose or homophonic cipher. A pass by an edge-table generator would favour generation.
   - m7 shows the h2 band itself moves with the alphabet (2.126–2.540), so the board must be judged within one transliteration.
2. **Is the transferable word order real, and does it need meaning?**
   - F1/F3 rest on residual excesses of 0.024–0.049 bits. The language flag clears its line by 0.007.
   - The global word-pair habit-table generator (F_RESULTS step 3) has not been tested. If it reproduces +0.04 on quire-split held-out text, the one test favouring encoded text stops discriminating. If it fails, the encoded-text family gains.
3. **What do line edges mean?**
   - m5 finds both a prefix-marker part (22.4% against a null of 10.9%) and a vocabulary part (JSD 0.140 among attested types).
   - A null-glyph marker favours a cipher. A vocabulary choice with no separable marker favours a generator or language layout.
   - Every producer fails here: c2, g1, h1, Naibbe, and T–S in the Voynich skeleton. So whatever mechanism fits m5's split, fixed in advance, is the most diagnostic next object.
4. **Is "spelling beats a word list" a generation signature or a cipher signature?**
   - The char model wins on Voynich (m3, by 0.094–0.526) and loses on Italian. Glyph position entropy is the lowest of any corpus (1.661), and Moran I is +0.141, stable across transliterations (m2).
   - None of these has been run on the F3/H1 cipher output or on the T–S and Schwartz outputs.
   - If a verbose homophonic cipher also shows char > word, position lock and Moran I > 0, these metrics stop pointing at generation. If only the generators show them, generation gains.
5. **Does any topic signal survive bifolio-aligned nulls, beyond quire drift?**
   - c1 left hand 1 at the minimum p (0.018) on 11 blocks and killed hand 2 (p=0.133). m0 shows same_page_far MI of +0.029 against about 0 for the controls.
   - A section or page signal that survives the bifolio null would favour content (NL or cipher). Its death would remove the last content-specific evidence and leave the encoded-text case on edge coupling and F1 alone.
