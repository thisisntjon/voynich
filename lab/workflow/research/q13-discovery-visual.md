# Q13 original-image observation: interrupted strand connectors

2026-10-19. Exploratory visual note, not a run, accepted finding, novelty claim, or semantic interpretation. Authority checked: upstream AGENTS.md, INVENTORY.md, and FINDINGS-MAP.md section 0. No canonical files changed. Only f79r and f79v were deliberately inspected, at overview and original resolution. f77v and other full Q13 images remain uninspected by this observer. The scans include narrow incidental fragments of adjacent leaves; these were not investigated or used as evidence.

## Outcome

There is a repeatable *general* graphic contrast between smooth paired-outline conduits and thin exposed bundles of strokes between outlined forms. Some exposed bundles include transverse bands of tiny detached marks or interruptions. However, these two pages do not establish a sufficiently precise new construction rule to support a discovery claim or a strong prediction. The strongest possible narrower proposal, that dotted interruptions mark a particular relation to figures or a particular type of join, is contradicted or left ambiguous by the variation described below. No holdout page was opened to rescue it.

“Connector,” “rim,” “container,” and “strand” below describe drawn geometry only. They do not imply function, contents, flow direction, anatomy, species, or meaning. Color describes visible paint in these scans, not an identified substance.

## What can and cannot be counted

The table identifies three long/narrow strand-link regions on f79r (upper rim-to-rim, central multi-tier, bottom terminal-to-green), plus five distinct f79v transition regions (top scalloped bundle, marks above reclining head, taper-to-blue opening, short blue collar link, bottom multi-lobed assembly-to-green). These are descriptive windows selected after looking, not an exhaustive or independently validated junction census. The five f79v regions explicitly include unlike constructions. They must not be reported as five instances of the hypothesized dotted motif.

The visually clearest detached-mark constructions are the central long bundle on f79r and the compact bundle above the reclining head on f79v. The lower f79r bundle supplies a less clear possible recurrence. Their figure relations differ: hands cross the f79r central bundle, a head lies below the f79v example, and the lower f79r interruption lacks immediate figure contact. Paint also differs within and between examples. Neither figure contact nor color yields a consistent specific rule here.

Tiny short marks should not automatically be transcribed as intentionally drawn dots. In the clearest f79v example, repeated rows of separated dark marks support a deliberate drawn feature rather than one accidental missing segment. On f79r, pale ink, overlapping hands, uneven paint and image reproduction can contribute to apparent breaks. No scan-to-scan comparison, pigment inspection, or physical examination was performed. “Dotted interruption” therefore remains a provisional visual description; individual mark counts and material causes are unresolved.

## Prediction decision

A weak falsifiable statement could be made: on a new page, detached transverse mark bands of this kind will occur in exposed thin-strand regions rather than inside smooth paired-outline conduits. But selecting “of this kind” from the same exposed regions risks making that statement circular. The feature definition is also vulnerable to annotator choices about dash versus faded line and where a smooth conduit ends. These pages do not support a stronger non-circular rule specifying the neighboring figure, container shape, color, or sequence position. Consequently this observation is not advanced as a new predictive candidate.

Independent annotation could probably distinguish broad smooth-wall and exposed-strand regions, but cannot yet be assumed to recover the narrower dotted motif reproducibly. That is a limitation of this observation, not an experiment performed or a new protocol proposed.

The f79v root/crown comparison with f90r1 is excluded: the lead identifies D'Imperio 1978 / c-581 as prior art. General Q13 text/image relations are also already publicly discussed (lead-supplied reference: Voynich Ninja thread 3771). No independent literature novelty clearance was attempted here. No statistics, model fitting, acquisition, physiological interpretation, or text-image alignment inference was performed.

### Continue/drop judgment

**DROP the generalized oval/rim–restart alignment lead on this exploratory evidence.** The selected f79r case is a real visual juxtaposition, and broad occupied-basin rims supply loose additional proximities on both pages. It is not uniquely isolated as a casual “near something” observation. But the *same specific construction* does not recur consistently: multiple clear restarts face plain strands, necks, tapering forms or the reclining figure, while many unmistakable rims face ordinary lines. The f79v hand-touched small rim is especially relevant negative context for the f79r hand-rim example.

Permitting a one-line or larger vertical tolerance and switching between an initial's top, its baseline, a broad rim's top, or its center could manufacture more matches. That flexibility also admits neighboring ordinary lines. This bounded pass therefore gives no actionable reason to open further pages or commission confirmation for this particular rule. Preserve the descriptive example and move to another lead; no significance, semantic link, or novelty is claimed.
