# Voynich working theory: a coordinated compilation, an unresolved textual purpose

2026-10-19. Private advisory synthesis requested by Jon; Astra. Existing evidence only. This document does not change a finding, grade, claim, decision, assignment or plan. Other manuscript work remains paused. Opus retains canonical custody; no additional review is assigned.

The strongest supported picture is a compilation whose makers shared an unusual writing convention, organized writing at several physical scales, and reused some pictorial material across sections. A reference or mnemonic work concerned with natural knowledge is a coherent purpose hypothesis. The evidence does not yet identify its users, geography, secrecy motive, language or a stable textual referent. The central puzzle is the coexistence of visual recurrence, strong textual construction rules and the failure of particular attempts to connect them. [F-01/F-04/F-15/F-16/F-19; L-355; F-20/c-590; c-581/c-583]

The improvement over the earlier syntheses is to separate three things that their stories sometimes merged: the physical units of production, the units governing written form, and the units carrying meaning. Lines constrain form; they have not been shown to be complete semantic entries. Bifolios can preserve production history without defining topics. Repeated drawings can preserve an association without being an index or establishing identical names. [F-01/F-04/F-51; P-87; L-216; L-355; F-20]

## Strongest reconstruction, with each bridge exposed

**Observation:** the surviving object combines plant-like drawings, diagrams, figures, containers and substantial writing on early-fifteenth-century parchment; its attributed hands share graphic conventions, while textual form responds to real spaces and lines. [c-561/c-583; F-19/F-01/F-04; c-581]

**Inference:** production involved a transmissible convention and coordination across units, whether by collaboration, copying exemplars or both; it is reasonable to treat the object as an organized compilation rather than presume spontaneous unrelated invention on each page. This does not establish simultaneous authorship or comprehension by every scribe. [c-583; F-15/F-16; L-355]

**Purpose hypothesis:** the book may have organized specialized natural knowledge for repeated consultation, with visual motifs helping a user recognize material and distinct textual formats doing different jobs. That supplies a possible use without demanding a continuous narrative or equating every label with an object name. It remains an inference from format and reuse, not an observed act of consultation. [L-355; c-132/L-216; F-20; c-581]

**Conjecture:** whole-plant depictions could present objects, fragment/container layouts could organize selected parts or uses, and the text-heavy ending could collect entries. Astral and conduit images could place those objects in a cosmological or physiological framework. These roles are compatible with the book; no operation, substance, ingredient, sentence or glyph value is thereby identified, and the biological figures have not been shown to mean plant processes. [L-355; c-581/H-20; L-216; c-578]

**Constraint:** whatever the content, any explanation must accommodate the writing's formal organization rather than assume ordinary prose was merely wrapped around pictures. It need not invent an additional avoidance process at line returns to explain the retired page-token-shuffle deficit. [F-04/F-15; L-190; P-87]

No evidence in this chain requires a secret order, medical household, one-time lesson, a single production campaign, one binding, or an unrecorded key sheet. Northern Italian medical concealment supplies a comparator; it does not locate or identify these makers. The geography and social story can remain open without weakening the established structural picture. [c-582/c-583; c-561; Zandbergen origin summary, Collation]

### Worked example: f18v, f23r and f102r2

**What we see:** the f18v root has a bulb above a horizontal structure with hooked appendages and a curled end; f23r has a segmented horizontal structure with ring-like ends and hanging rootlets. The corresponding root motifs occur beside one another in the bottom row of f102r2, fragments 212/213. Catalogue and P05/P06 eye-check records identify the matching region as root-only; neither supplies a species name. f18v/f23r also occupy one bifolio side. [L-355; plants/facts.tsv:643–652; eyecheck/pairs.tsv:P05/P06]

**Reference interpretation:** a user could recognize the same visual objects in whole-plant and selected-part contexts. Reusing the pair might preserve a useful association from an exemplar or earlier arrangement. 'Picture citation' is an apt hypothesis for this behavior, provided it does not imply a known direction or a proven index. [L-355, inference]

**Challenge from text:** the tested nearby pharma strings, `koldarod` and `odalydary`, have no edit-distance<=1 matches in their corresponding herbal body texts in the P05/P06 ZL rows. The overall test is powered for its stated similar-name model, not every possible encoding. Thus we cannot say 'these names identify the earlier plants', but also cannot conclude 'the words must be operations'. [F-20/c-590; results_s128_20261019T090659.txt:26–27]

**Strongest rival:** a maker reused drawing templates while producing separate patterned text. A shared written-and-drawn exemplar could also preserve associations even if a copyist did not understand them. The pair and target panel helped generate the hypothesis; they cannot independently test it. Seven stronger matches all share physical folio 102, so the proposed grouped body-text test already failed eligibility. This example is a worked interpretation of existing evidence, not a reopened study. [L-355; F-20; plant-group eligibility disposition]

## Challenge, novelty and the one missing piece

The weakest indispensable assumption of the meaningful-reference account is **that a textual convention carries stable content, rather than only production state, visual role or learned surface regularity**. Even container-versus-root classification would not by itself prove semantics: a filler procedure could switch modes by caption role. The strongest competitor is C, a coherent visual compilation with structured writing that lacks recoverable textual reference. [F-20/F-33/F-43; c-550; c-623; H-05/H-07]

Removing the published partial-object recurrences in **L-355** would most weaken the reference-use reconstruction: section variety and formal writing alone provide little reason to infer links between pictured entries. Removing **F-04** would most change the production account, but would not resolve meaning. No accepted result presently binds these two kinds of evidence into a decoded relation. [L-355; F-04; F-20/F-43]

Established prior work includes positional writing effects, patterned word construction, label registers, multiple-hand analyses, broad length autocorrelation, the visual correspondences and the cosmic–medical interpretation. SSR's narrower contributions include measured comparisons, the audited learned localization, s131's quantified recombination effect and P-87's scoped correction. Exact first-ever priority remains unestablished. Llull, Species Plantarum and self-citation controls constrain universal claims, but use different statistics or limited source families. The knowledge web is a demonstrated resource contribution, not evidence for this purpose hypothesis. [L-370/x01-published-first.md; L-189/L-190; c-623; P-87; c-581; clear-wins science and synthesis reviews]

**One discriminating observation to recommend, not execute:** an independently grounded, reusable mapping from a Voynich textual form to a *specific referent or technical relation*, fixed before evaluation, that correctly predicts that referent/relation on a separate, genuinely unexposed physical sheet beyond matched production/layout alternatives. It must predict content rather than merely label length, caption class or section. A compositional linguistic recovery would favor B; a nonlinguistic but stable reference/operation relation would favor A; either would challenge C's production-only writing. A copy from a meaningful exemplar would remain possible, and no whole-book translation would follow.

Existing evidence does not supply that observation: F-20's tested name transfer is null; F-43 lacks representation robustness; s125 never reached a Voynich comparison; db-S141 is external calibration; c-581 supplies interpretation rather than a bilingual key. The grouped folio-102 proposal is closed for eligibility and is not the recommended test. The inspected prior-work reviews identify no accepted reading that supplies the mapping, but this is not an exhaustive claim about all scholarship. [c-590/c-597/c-598/c-578/c-586/c-581; eligibility disposition; B-prior-art]

Required evidence would be an independently justified referent/role, a single fixed decoding or indexing rule, a documented exposure ledger, and an eligible separate physical unit. No such eligible package is identified here. Any pattern chosen from the worked example is retrospective; relabeling old pages as held out does not make it untouched. Therefore the recommendation is a **reopening condition**, not a runnable new compute assignment. The pause remains appropriate. A reliable powered failure on unused predictions rejects the specified mapping and weakens that version of A or B; one null cannot reject every meaningful manuscript. An uninterpretable or unpowered outcome leaves the tie intact.

**What do these pieces let us understand together that we could not see separately?** They support an organized visual compilation and a shared writing practice whose constraints operate at different scales; they also show exactly why neither formal regularity nor failed plant-name cribs establishes its meaning. **Which connections are solid, which are provisional, and what specific observation would tell us whether this picture is right?** Formal word/line organization, scoped motif recurrence and the corrected test outcomes are solid within their methods. Reference use, section unity, textual semantics and historical motive are provisional. A fixed, independently grounded textual relation that predicts content on an unused sheet would join the two halves of the puzzle.
