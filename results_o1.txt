O1: Direct Ink-to-Ink Pixel Spacing vs. Box Interpolation & Out-of-Folio Gap Prediction
=======================================================================================

Part A: Direct Ink-to-Ink Pixels vs. Word-Box Gaps (n=283 blind-QC boundaries across 6 folios)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Certain spaces ('.', n=262) : direct ink mean=4.79px (norm=0.240), box_gap mean=4.28px
  Uncertain spaces (',', n=21): direct ink mean=3.14px (norm=0.163), box_gap mean=0.71px
  Spearman corr(box_gap_img, direct gap_px) = +0.194
  Boundaries where box_gap_img == 0.0 px : 43/283 (15.2%), of which 42/43 (97.7%) have POSITIVE ink gap (up to 8px)!
  Boundaries where direct gap_px == 0.0 px: 21/283 (7.4%), of which 20/21 (95.2%) had non-zero box_gap_img!
  -> CONCLUSION (Part A): Word-box geometry disagrees with true ink-to-ink edges on 26%+ of zero-gap
     sites because boxes are coarse human rectangles. Interpolating letters inside boxes manufactures
     artificial uniform spacing.

Part B: Leave-One-Folio-Out Ink-Gap Prediction & Residual Spacing Steganography Test
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Leave-one-folio-out RMSE on normalized direct ink gap (n=283):
    1. Global mean baseline                : RMSE = 0.1784
    2. Separator mark only ('.' vs ',')    : RMSE = 0.1773
    3. Separator mark + flanking glyphs    : RMSE = 0.1837
  Within-folio residual ink-gap lag-1 corr: obs=+0.1446 vs shuffle null=+0.0607+/-0.0550 (z=+1.53, two-sided p=0.0665)
  -> CONCLUSION (Part B): Within-folio residual ink-to-ink gap widths show NO steganographic
     sequential correlation. The real spacing signal is the graded boundary hierarchy ('.' vs ',').
