2A. Methodological Specification (R&T 2026 Code & Text vs. Our c3 / m0 / s20 / s30):
  1. Transliteration & Locus Filter:
     - R&T 2026 (reproduce_edge_order.py): Parses ZL3b.txt (ZL3b-n.txt), keeps only primary
       paragraph loci (typ == 'P', no ';tr' interlinear code), strips <!...>, <...>, {...},
       replaces [a:b] -> a, removes apostrophes, splits on '.' and ',' (or '.' only for the
       weak-spaces-merged row), and SKIPS ANY LINE where ANY token fails re.fullmatch(r'[a-z]+').
       Result on ZL3b: 3,950 paragraph lines kept, 80 lines with @nnn; / ? / * skipped entirely,
       32,747 tokens, 28,797 adjacent intra-line pairs.
     - Our vparse / c3 (score.py): Parses data/ZL3b-n.txt P-loci, strips markup, drops only
       individual uncertain tokens containing '?' (rather than discarding the whole line).
       Result on ZL3b: 4,135 paragraph lines with >=2 tokens, 34,475 tokens, 30,342 pairs.
  2. Alphabet Reduction:
     - R&T Primary ('collapsed EVA'): Collapses 9 multi-character EVA sequences into single
       glyph symbols before taking word-final and word-initial characters:
         cth->T, ckh->K, cph->P, cfh->F, ch->C, sh->S, iin->N, in->I, ee->E.
     - R&T Sensitivity ('raw / decomposed EVA') & Our c3/m0/s30: Uses raw single-character EVA
       (w[-1] -> w'[0]). R&T Table 11 also reports 2-glyph edges (w[-2:] -> w'[:2]).
  3. Logarithm Base & Estimator:
     - Both R&T and our c3/m0/s30 use base-2 logarithm (BITS) and the plug-in pair MI
       I(X;Y) = H(X) + H(Y) - H(X,Y) over the observed adjacent intra-line pair marginals.
     - R&T additionally reports the normalized ratio 100 * excess_MI / H(first1) (%).
  4. Null Model:
     - R&T 2026: Mean of 100 within-line token permutations (np.random.default_rng(20260816)).
     - Our c3 (score.py cross_excess_eva): Mean of 20 within-line token permutations (Random(17)).
     - Our m0 (metrics_new.py x_edge_mi_word): Mean of 5 global pair permutations (default_rng(0)),
       permuting right-hand tokens across the whole corpus (leaves ~0.018 bits of line/page pooling).
     - Our s30 (s30_phase2_u2_u3_deltas.py): 200 within-line AND 200 (hand, section, slot) shuffles.
