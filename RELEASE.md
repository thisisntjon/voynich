Read a cropped Yale label alongside both transcriptions, fold the first gathering, or try to reunite anonymous fragments. [Start with one handwritten label](https://github.com/thisisntjon/voynich/blob/reconstruction-atlas-v1/START-READING.md).

**Voynich-Reconstruction-Atlas-1.0.zip** contains the full offline reader, 204 manuscript photographs, 61 fragment puzzles, all 707 matched-character word-division disagreements, and the research downloads. Extract it and open `index.html` in a browser.

**atlas-data-v1.zip** contains the SQLite catalog of 266,639 manuscript parts and research tables. **replication-v1.zip** contains the inputs, source code, reference predictions and checks for the visual and text reassembly experiments. **fold-quire-1.pdf** contains printable sheets for recreating the first gathering.

The photographs, transcriptions and geometry sources retain their individual terms. Courtesy of the Yale University Library, Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, MS 408.
