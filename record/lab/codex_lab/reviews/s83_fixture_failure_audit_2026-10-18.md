## Result

S83 combines homophonic letter/syllable/chunk-bearing cores with core-dependent, line-sticky affix tables, cadence, and local homophone reuse. Its positive control generated a synthetic manuscript from that same family and attempted four leave-one-quire-out recoveries.

- Configuration selection identified the planted Latin-syllable/cadence family on 3/4 folds.
- The primary fixture gate required the selected model to reproduce at least three of P1 rhythm, P2 core order, P3 prefix gain, and P7 vocabulary size on at least three folds.
- It succeeded on only 1/4 folds. The fixed planted configuration also succeeded on only 1/4.
- The negative Naibbe control failed as expected on 4/4 folds, and GPU/CPU matched-inventory MI agreed, so those checks passed.
- Final fixture verdict: `FAIL`.

## What failed

Vocabulary size transferred best (`P7` on 4/4 selected folds). The other planted properties did not transfer reliably:

- P1 rhythm: 1/4 folds.
- P2 core order: 1/4 folds.
- P3 prefix gain: 2/4 folds.
- Matched-K core-order C2: 0/4 folds.
- R(1) plus s69 C3: 0/4 folds; selected mean R(1) was `0.741`, below the required `>1`.

The fixture also exposes two construction-level problems before any Voynich scoring. Every configuration has negative `CHARWIN` on the synthetic target, and the proposed same-unit homophone recency does not produce excess short-range repetition of the same operational core. Those two properties are load-bearing Voynich constraints.

The refitted fold models also change the induced segmentation and homophone allocation. The all-page planted pool had 2,335 cores with homophone median 1 and maximum 14, while fold refits had roughly 1,936–2,107 cores with median 3 and maxima 6–7. The family is therefore not stable under its own train/held-out inference procedure.

## Board decision

Do not freeze or run the first s83 version on the real manuscript. The fixture failure is not evidence against every hybrid cipher; it rejects that implementation and inference procedure. The revised oracle-ceiling fixture must be judged as a new positive control and must not reinterpret the first result retroactively.

A successor should be attempted only if it can meet all three requirements on unseen planted folds:

1. fold-stable operational segmentation and homophone assignment without access to held-out pages;
2. positive `CHARWIN` generated by the construction rather than supplied by a wrapper;
3. same-core short-range recurrence `R(1) > 1`, implemented as core-level state rather than reuse conditional on a rarely repeated plaintext unit.

The next positive control should preserve the global-manuscript generation design. Generating each held-out fixture fold directly from its training-fold model would make the recovery test circular.
