# Public herbal, pharmaceutical, zodiac, and alchemical-herbal catalogues

Stamped 2026-10-18 21:08:57 -07:00 (`Get-Date`). Picture identifications below are crib candidates for a test. They are not readings of the Voynich text. No images or books were downloaded. O-062 is the missing second herbal whose per-folio identities would let a plant-match test be frozen. s115-A0 already used LJS 419, Bodleian Canon. Misc. 408, and Sloane 4016.

## Which public plant identifications exist for Beinecke MS 408, and are they a folio-plus-binomial table under a usable licence?

### Takeaway
Several public lists pair a herbal folio with a proposed plant, but none opened here is both a confirmed folio-plus-binomial table and an open machine-readable file that is only an identification. Sherwood's three web lists are the main picture cribs; Tucker and Talbert 2013 and the Janick books are copyrighted New-World proposals; Touwaide 2015 is a comparison essay, not a species table. No Taiz and Taiz table was found.

### Cited Findings
- Zandbergen groups picture comparisons with nature (Dana Scott, Edith Sherwood, Steve "D") separately from comparisons with other herbals (Ellie Velinska, Sherwood). He does not call any of them a settled binomial catalogue. — [voynich.nu illustrations](https://www.voynich.nu/illustr.html)
- Sherwood's pages, as linked from the bibliography: version 1, comparison with nature, [voynich_botanical_plants](http://www.edithsherwood.com/voynich_botanical_plants/index.php); version 2, comparison with other herbals, [voynich_botanical_plants_2](http://www.edithsherwood.com/voynich_botanical_plants_2/index.php); version 3, which "includ[es] tentative plant name decryptions from the text", [voynich-botanical-plant-anagrams](http://www.edithsherwood.com/voynich-botanical-plant-anagrams/index.php). Version 3 is a claimed reading, not a picture crib. The live site was not opened; no licence statement was retrieved. Sherwood version 1 or 2, if the HTML can be recovered and its licence cleared, is still the picture list. It was already the local crib and does not by itself supply the second herbal O-062 needs.

### Gaps

- The PDF tables inside the Springer and Wiley books were not opened.

## Do Bodleian Canon. Misc. 408 and UPenn LJS 419 put per-folio plant names in the IIIF manifest or the catalogue, and what does Beasley 2024 say about the stemma?

### Takeaway
Canon. Misc. 408 has machine-readable per-folio caption names in the IIIF ranges, not modern binomials, and the catalogue does not list plants folio by folio. LJS 419's catalogue and manifest, as checked for s103, do not. Beasley 2024 puts LJS 419 in a proposed alchemical-herbal sub-tradition; Philip Neal's summary of Segre Rutz, not Beasley, is what places Canon. Misc. 408 in the main tradition.

### Cited Findings
- Canon. Misc. 408, Digital Bodleian viewer [db2fade4-61ee-4a11-a894-19361c551eed](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/objects/db2fade4-61ee-4a11-a894-19361c551eed/); IIIF manifest [same id](https://iiif.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/iiif/manifest/db2fade4-61ee-4a11-a894-19361c551eed.json); catalogue [manuscript_3439](https://medieval.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/catalog/manuscript_3439). Manifest metadata: title "Herbal.", Italy, Latin, "15th century, end." Attribution string read from the saved manifest: "Photo: (c) Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford. Terms of use: CC BY-NC 4.0" with [the CC licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) and [Digital Bodleian terms](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/terms/). A range label in that file is `fol. 001r: Herba Antolla minor`. The project README, from the same manifest, says 167 ranges over 166 canvases (slides, fol. 001r-085r, gaps), and that the catalogue text gives no per-folio plant names. — manifest as saved under `voynich/data/herbals_alchemical/bodleian_canon_misc_408/`; README there

## BnF Latin 17848 and 17844, BL Add. 41623, BSB Clm 5905: open facsimile and a folio-level plant index?

### Takeaway
Add. 41623 and Clm 5905 have open facsimiles. Only Clm 5905 has a folio-level index, and it is a partial German name list for a Bavarian Macer herbal, not the alchemical tradition. The two Paris manuscripts are catalogued as public domain, but Gallica was down earlier on 2026-10-18 and neither catalogue has a per-folio plant index. Segre Rutz 2000 is still the printed index.

### Cited Findings
- Lat. 17844: Gallica ark `btv1b10032359h`, catalogue [cc688391](https://archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc688391), rights recorded as "domaine public", digitised from a microfilm. Lat. 17848: arks `btv1b104651858` and `btv1b10036013d`. The s105 README (13:48-14:12 PDT the same day) says every Gallica fetch failed (403, Cloudflare, or HTTP 500 on the ARK) and that the catalogue cites Segre Rutz 2000 and Collins 2000 but has no per-folio plant list. This pass did not re-test Gallica. — `bnf_lat17844/README.md`, `bnf_lat17848/README.md`
- Segre Rutz, *Il giardino magico degli alchimisti* (Milan, 2000), is the critical edition of Pavia Aldini 211, with pictures of that manuscript and a verbal description of the others. Neal also notes Lupo's edition of the Trent manuscript and Ragazzini's edition of a Florence manuscript. None of these was found as an open data file. — [Neal](http://philipneal.net/voynichsources/alchemical/); [refs.html](https://www.voynich.nu/refs.html)
- BL Add. MS 41623, "Codex Bellunensis", catalogue [032-002085314](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-002085314), IIIF [bl.digirati.io ark](https://bl.digirati.io/iiif/ark:/81055/vdc_100165149757.0x000001). Manifest usage terms, as recorded: "Public Domain in most countries other than the UK", rights page [BL unpublished manuscripts](https://www.bl.uk/help/how-to-reuse-images-of-unpublished-manuscripts). Catalogue identities found: Edelweiss on f. 35v, and Carline thistle ("Oculis bovis", folio not stated). No per-folio list. The catalogue calls it a Dioscorides adaptation. Beasley's 38-manuscript list, as quoted locally, does not include it. — `bl_add41623/README.md`; Beasley URL above
- BSB Clm 5905, viewer [bsb00092488](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/view/bsb00092488), IIIF [manifest](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb00092488/manifest), licence Public Domain Mark 1.0 ([CC PDM](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)). Catalogue title is a Macer herbal drawn at Ebersberg in 1479. The manifest TOC has 178 illustration ranges; 72 carry a modern German species name and 106 do not. Beasley, as quoted, does not list this shelfmark. — `bsb_clm5905/README.md`

### Inferences
- Clm 5905 is the only one of the four with both an open facsimile and a machine-readable (partial) plant index. It cannot be the same-plant pair for Canon. Misc. 408. Add. 41623 does not close O-062. The Paris manuscripts would, if Gallica were up and if Segre's folio concordance were in hand; neither is available as open data.

### Gaps
- Gallica was not re-tested after 14:12 PDT.
- No open digitisation of Segre Rutz 2000, Lupo, or Ragazzini was found.
- Mayer, Goehl and Englert (2009) on the Clm 5905 pictures was named locally and not opened.

## Zodiac month names: Zandbergen, Pelling, any 2020-2026 revision, and a month-unique Voynichese word?

### Takeaway
Zandbergen's table on the live site still reads the Latin-script glosses as Northern French month names. No 2020-2026 change to those readings was on that page. No study consulted here reports a Voynichese word that is unique to one month. These glosses are not Voynichese and are not a decipherment.

### Cited Findings
- Table read on [writing.html](https://www.voynich.nu/writing.html): f70v2 Pisces `mars`; f70v1 and f71r Aries `aberil`; f71v and f72r1 Taurus `may`; f72r2 Gemini `jong`; f72r3 Cancer `iollet`; f72v3 Leo `augst`; f72v2 Virgo `septe(m)b(r)`; f72v1 Libra `octe(m)bre`; f73r Scorpius `nove(m)bre`; f73v Sagittarius `decebre`. He calls them a later hand, argues Northern French from `jong`, and cites similar month names on a Northern French astrolabe. The page copyright is his, 2004-2026. A note dated 27/03/2026 ([notice.html](https://www.voynich.nu/notice.html)) does not revise the month names.
- The same page does not claim that any Voynichese word is month-unique. The word "unique" there refers to the script, not to a month token.
- A local note opened this session attributes to Pelling "some form of Occitan" and cites [Jaume Deydier's livre de raison](https://ciphermysteries.com/2009/08/22/jaume-deydiers-livre-de-raison). That page was not re-fetched. The same note records no published month-word match (Zandbergen's [labels study](https://voynich.nu/extra/labels.html); ninja thread [5299](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-5299.html)). — `research_notes/External anchors/zodiac_months.md` lines 9-22 and 72-78

### Inferences
- The glosses are an owner's Latin-alphabet crib for the zodiac pages only. They do not identify a Voynichese month word, which matches the local test already run. Nothing from 2020-2026 found here changes the readings enough to reopen that test.

### Gaps
- Pelling's page and any 2024-2026 revision outside Zandbergen's site were not re-read. OpenAlex title search for "Voynich" 2018-2026 was dominated by unrelated Zenodo decipherment uploads and was not a reliable revision list.

## Is there a public pharmaceutical jar list (folio, jar, proposed contents) newer than the local crib notes?

### Takeaway
No such list was found. Zandbergen classifies pages as jars; he does not name the contents. Early pharmaceutical identifications are described as rare and unpublished on his herb-id page.

### Cited Findings

- [herb_oldid.html](https://www.voynich.nu/extra/herb_oldid.html): "Some ID's have also been given for the pharmaceutical pages, but these are rare, and may be presented in a different form later."

The abstract was not opened, so it is not evidence of a folio-jar-contents table. — [OpenAlex work W7128147250](https://openalex.org/W7128147250)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## Which open northern-Italian alchemical herbals have both images and a transcription if Gallica is down?

### Takeaway
None confirmed here has both a complete open facsimile and a scholarly transcription. Canon. Misc. 408 has open (CC BY-NC) images and machine-readable caption names, which is the page's own label, not Segre's edition. The witnesses Neal lists besides Paris were not confirmed as digitized with a text.

### Cited Findings
- Zandbergen: the alchemical-herbal tradition is mainly northern Italy, late 14th century to the first half of the 16th, "mostly fantastical", one or sometimes two plants per page; the main reference is Segre Rutz 2000; many copies also contain a *Tractatus de herbis* selection. Toresella in the 1990s compared the Voynich pictures with that tradition. — [illustr.html](https://www.voynich.nu/illustr.html)
- Neal's digitized-status is not stated. He names, among others, Pavia Aldini 211 (the manuscript Segre edited), Bodleian Canon. Misc. 408 (images and caption labels, above), Bodleian Add. A. 23, Wellcome 261, 334, 337, Florence Biblioteca di Botanica MS 106, Laurenziana Ashb. 456, Marucelliana 168/C, and Marciana It. III.11 (5004) and It. II.12 (4936). Editions he names are printed: Segre Rutz 2000, Lupo (Trent), Ragazzini (Florence). — [Neal](http://philipneal.net/voynichsources/alchemical/)
- Marco Ponzi's 2016 voynich.ninja thread is described in the 408 README as a list of 98 tradition names with Segre's identifications in parentheses and links to 408 images; six rows are missing from 408; the folio for each row is not printed to guests. Forum post, no licence, not an edition. — README citing [thread 485](https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=485)
- Clm 5905 and Add. 41623 are open but are the wrong books for this tradition (see previous section). The two Paris copies, which Neal says are the two copies of lost "y", are the Gallica pair that was down.

### Inferences
- O-062 is not closed by a second open transcription. The nearest Gallica-free object is still Canon. Misc. 408's CC BY-NC caption list, already used. The next witness to check, because Neal puts it in the same tradition and it is not Paris, is one of Wellcome 261 / 334 / 337, Bodleian Add. A. 23, Pavia Aldini 211, or a Florence or Venice copy, and only if its own folio labels or an open edition can be cited. Clm 5905's German TOC must not be used as that pair.

### Gaps
- Open IIIF and any folio index for Wellcome 261, 334, 337, Bodleian Add. A. 23, Pavia Aldini 211, Laurenziana Ashb. 456, the other Florence copies, the two Marciana shelfmarks, and the Vicenza copy of lat. 17844. Not verified.
- No open machine-readable text of Segre Rutz's edition was found.
