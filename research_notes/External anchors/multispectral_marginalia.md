# The 2024 multispectral release and non-Voynichese writing as external anchors

Notes written 2026-10-17 20:03 PDT (clock read with `date`). Read-only web research. No images were downloaded. Quotes are kept to a few words per source.

Related local items:

- `research_notes/Voynich decipherment attempts and failures/physical_provenance.md`, Q5 and Q6.

## Bottom line

**No source supplies a provenanced crib.** Nothing in the release, or in later discussion through October 2026, pairs Voynich glyphs with known letters or words on authority older than a 17th-century reader.

- **f1r columns.** The only glyph-to-letter pairing is the set of f1r margin columns.
  - Who and when: Davis attributes them to Marci, who owned the book 1662-1665. Others propose an earlier owner, or an original glyph column with Latin letters added later.
  - Alignment: the columns are not cleanly row-aligned.
  - Published table: none in text form. Davis's "preliminary transcription" is posted only as an image.
  - Tests so far: forum users who applied the pairing as a substitution report garbage.
- **f116v and f17r.** These contain a few embedded Voynichese words, for example `<oror sheey>` and `<otaiol oim>`. They carry no gloss, so they are not glyph-to-letter cribs.

## Q1. What is legible now

### f1r: the three (or four) margin columns

**Layout.** The three columns are:
1. A Roman alphabet, a to z.
2. A series of Voynich characters.
3. A second Roman alphabet "offset by one letter".

Davis notes that a to e are clearly visible and that g and h are legible only in column 3. Some column-2 symbols remain unclear. — [Davis, 8 Sep 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/)

Forum readers add the following (forum observations, unverified):
- A partial fourth column adds k and l, possibly also m and n. — asteckley, 15 Sep 2024 ([thread 4363 p5](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-5.html))
- The k and l are in a "smaller", more recent hand. — nablator ([thread 4363 p4](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-4.html))

**Alignment.** The columns are not clean rows.
- Stolfi's Note 040 records that the columns are "not well synchronized". Its partial pairs come from the 1994 readings by Reeds and Zandbergen and are only partly consistent, unverified: a~EVA d, c~EVA r, h~EVA y (Zandbergen), y~g (Reeds), and a gallows near q-s. — [Stolfi Note 040, f1r](https://ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/040/html/f1r.htm)
- A vertical wormhole forced the a, b and c entries to the right, joined by connecting strokes. — asteckley ([thread 4363 p3](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-3.html))

**Who made it.**
- **Marci, 1662-1665** (Davis, with Zandbergen agreeing on the hand). Her letterform markers include loopless b, d, f, h, p, q, s and y, an open-bowl g, and the shapes of m and z. She compared them with Marci's autograph letter of 12 Sep 1640 (APUG 557 f.127r). — [Davis 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/); [thread 4363](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363.html)
- **Dissent (forum, unverified):**
  - pfeaster suggests the glyph column is by a different, earlier person than the Latin columns. — [p4](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-4.html)
  - asteckley conjectures this writing order: column 2 first, then column 3, then column 4, then column 1. — [p5](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-5.html)
  - oshfdk argues the Voynichese column is original and that Marci only added the letters. — [thread 6027, Aug 2026](https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=6027)
  - Stolfi suggests Barschius rather than Marci, citing Marci's poor eyesight. — [thread 6027](https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=6027)
- **Ink.** Davis says the columns are "almost certainly" iron-gall. Only XRF could compare the inks. — [p3](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-3.html)

**Glyph segmentation.** Davis reports that the column treats EVA [an] and [in] as single characters. RobGea counters that [an] is rare (count 122) while [in] is common (count 6044). — [thread 4363](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363.html)

**Davis's view.** The sequence is "not in any obvious order" and does not match the glyph sequences on f49v, f57v or f66r. The alphabets "will likely not help" decipherment. — [Davis 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/)

### f116v marginalia

**The 2024 blog post.** It gives no word readings, only letter changes:
- The 3rd and 4th words begin with "perhaps u or v" rather than p.
- Faint lines are stains.
- The writing has "Germanic features" and is similar to, but not quite, Voynichese.
- The hand is roughly contemporary with the main text or a little later.

— [Davis 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/)

**Current best reading.** This is Davis's draft, posted on the forum on 25 Jan 2026, which she marks as uncertain and calls "pseudo-Germanic". Unverified against the images; I give only short fragments:
- line 1 begins "pox leber"
- line 2 has "anchiton oladabas" in place of Newbold's "michiton"
- line 3 has "ma+ria"
- line 4 begins with Voynichese `<oror sheey>` and ends "so nim gas mich"

— [thread 4364 p11](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4364-11.html). The forum repost is from a draft of her forthcoming book; she dates the inscription perhaps late 15th century. — [thread 4364 p12 (via search)](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4364-12.html), unverified

Other points on f116v:
- **"pox leber".** Read by many as Alemannic/Bavarian "goat liver". The reading is disputed. — [thread 4894](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4894.html) (forum, via search snippet, unverified)
- **"valden".** Not found in any 2024-2026 source. The closest is "Valde ub vento" in a 2012 amateur reading. — local file `ciphermysteries_f116v_p4559.html.txt` line 47 ([Pelling 2012](https://ciphermysteries.com/2012/04/21/yamdtatth-yet-another-michitonese-decipherment-to-add-to-the-heap))
- **Stolfi.** The signs are mostly deformed Latin letters plus some Voynichese-like ones, with no "plausible coherent parsing". — [thread 4364 p11](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4364-11.html). His notes argue the text is a later "reconstruction" over erased Voynichese. — [Stolfi Note 075 f116v](https://ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/Notes/075/report/f116v1/page.html) (via search snippet, unverified)
- **Pelling.** Since 2012 he has held that much of f116v was "overwritten by a later owner". — local file line 55

## Q2. Does any reading pair Voynich glyphs with known letters?

- **f1r.** This is the only explicit pairing, but it is weak in three ways:
  - Its provenance is a 17th-century reader, or at best an unknown owner, not the author.
  - The rows are poorly aligned and several glyphs are illegible.
  - Two Latin alphabets offset by one letter leave ambiguous which pairing was meant.
- **f116v and f17r.** Voynichese tokens (`oror sheey`, `otaiol oim`) sit inside lines in Latin script, with no translation. Their only test value is a weak one: these words may show that a near-contemporary writer knew the script. Pelling calls this a "direct link". — [Cipher Mysteries, Jan 2026](https://ciphermysteries.com/2026/01/22/finally-i-can-read-the-voynich-manuscript). The neighbouring Latin-script words are not glosses of them.

## Q3. Expert views and the strongest objection

- **Davis.** The release gives no key; the alphabets will likely not help. — [Davis 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/)
- **Zandbergen.** He reads the columns as a first simple-substitution attempt, later replaced by an unfinished second alphabet. He asked whether it yields "anything reasonable" but did not test it. — [thread 4363 p2](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-2.html)
- **Pelling.** He has posted no f1r analysis that I found; his 2024 archive has no September post. — [ciphermysteries.com/2024/11](https://ciphermysteries.com/2024/11) (absence not confirmed)
- **Strongest objection.** The pairing is a later owner's guess. Marci, or Barschius, had no access to the author's system, so agreement with it proves nothing about the cipher. The partial fourth column and the offset alphabet also show the writer revising.
- **Second objection.** Simple substitution is already excluded by the text's statistics. — [Bluetoes101, thread 6027](https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=6027)

## Q4. Is there a testable published glyph-by-letter table?

**Not in text form.** The candidate sources:
- **Davis's preliminary transcription.** It exists only as an image in the 2024 post. — [Davis 2024](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/)
- **Forum page checks.** Pages 1-5 of thread 4363 and thread 6027 contain no typed letter-to-EVA list, and the forum hides images from guests.
- **Partial pairs.** pfeaster's few hypotheses, for example f/g→[in], and Stolfi's 1994 pairs are fragments.

Substitution tests already reported:
- **Koen G**, 9 Sep 2024: "garbage in - garbage out". — [p2](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4363-2.html)
- **exlipsiae**, Aug 2026: applied the column-1 and column-3 keys to the full text. The results were a few chance words ("rex", "bleak"), "even less promising" with column 3. — [thread 6027](https://www.voynich.ninja/printthread.php?tid=6027)

**Building a test for us** means transcribing the table by hand from Davis's image (a human step, consistent with O-030), then encoding both alignments (column 1 and column 3) as substitution maps. Expect failure. Its value would be as a negative control, not as an anchor.
