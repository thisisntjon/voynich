## Cappelli abbreviation dictionary — VERIFIED AVAILABLE

Adriano Cappelli, Lexicon Abbreviaturarum (1899; 1912/1929 eds.),
~14,000 medieval Latin/Italian abbreviation forms. MEMSLib (Univ. Zurich
palaeography guide, memslib.co.uk/palaeography) states a full scan is on
Archive.org. Known limits (Alberta MS-course guide): almost exclusively
Italian manuscripts; dating unreliable outside Italy.
Team use: abbreviation-expansion control corpus requires OCR/extraction
from scan (image PDF) — medium effort, fully agent-executable.
Register claim "Cappelli-class = closest shorthand neighbour" remains
their Supported judgment, not verified here.

## Quid pro quo substitution tables — GENRE VERIFIED, 1477 ITEM UNVERIFIED

Genre is real and old: Pseudo-Galen De succedaneis tradition; Lorsch
Pharmacopoeia antemballomena list; School of Salernum Quid pro Quo text
freely available as plain text (archive.org djvu.txt, e.g.
"Pro abrotano, absynthium vel origanum") — agent-downloadable today and
directly usable as a substitution-table control.
The specific register claim (1477 Bologna Benedetto incunabulum embeds a
printed two-column table) traces only to the register author's own
Zenodo paper — self-citation. Treat 1477 item as UNVERIFIED until an
ISTC/GW incunabulum record or library digitisation is opened.
