Third-party material is not covered by either licence and is not included. Transliterations, manuscript scans, comparison corpora, papers and models are downloaded by `tools/fetch_data.py` and the other `tools/fetch_*.py` scripts, and keep their owners' terms. `data/SOURCES.tsv` lists each source and what we know about its licence. Short quotations of published work inside our notes are attributed to their authors. Some results files and tables quote transliterated words and lines as examples (folio labels in `runs/s116/units.tsv`, glyph word lists under `data/physical/glyphs/`, quoted spans in a few `results_*.txt`; together under 3% of the transliteration); those are from the Zandbergen–Landini (ZL), Takahashi (IT) or reference (RF) transliterations at https://www.voynich.nu/ and remain their makers' work. Our data tables cite third-party pages by link; quotation and abstract columns were removed before publication.

One exception to "not included": `runs/s108/` keeps short OCR extracts (about 180 KB) of printed books from 1531–32, which are in the public domain, because the run's results are read from them. They come from archive.org scans and are not covered by our licences.
